Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Bubblodile ("the game") is a water-toy game for Android made by Token Limit Games (Aleksandr Sandler), Lithuania. Contact: tokenlimitgames@gmail.com.

This policy explains what data the game and the services it uses handle, and what choices you have.

The short version

  • The game has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your personal bests, settings and chosen creature are stored only on your phone.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device data (such as the advertising ID) to show and measure ads. In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland you're asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in the game.
  • The developer does not sell, receive or store your personal data.

Data stored on your phone

The game saves your personal bests (fastest time, fewest pumps), the sound setting, the creature you picked, and which shop items you own, in the app's private storage on your device. It is never sent to the developer. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data. Android's own backup may include it if you have device backup turned on.

Motion sensors and vibration

The game reads your phone's motion sensors (gravity and acceleration) so that tilting and shaking the phone moves the water and marbles. These readings are used only on your phone, while you play; they are not stored or sent anywhere. The game also uses vibration for button and gulp effects.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows a banner ad, provided by Google AdMob. To serve, measure and prevent fraud in ads, Google may collect and process data from your device, such as:

  • the advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, app version),
  • interactions with ads.

If you consent (or where consent isn't required), ads may be personalized. If you don't consent, Google serves non-personalized ads, which still use limited data (for example for frequency capping and fraud prevention). Learn more in Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices:

  • EU, EEA, UK, Switzerland: you're asked for consent the first time you open the game. You can change it any time via "Privacy options" in the game's About screen.
  • Everywhere: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Buying Remove ads in the shop removes the ads.

Google Play services

  • In-app purchases (Remove ads and creatures) are handled by Google Play. The developer receives a confirmation of purchase, not your payment details.
  • Google's handling of this data is described in Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Sharing pictures

When you tap Share after a game, the game creates a picture of the toy with your result. It is passed to the app you choose only when you ask for it. The developer never receives it.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Ads are not intended for children.

Your rights (EU/EEA/UK)

Since the developer does not collect or store your personal data, there's nothing the developer holds about you. For data processed by Google (ads, Play), you can exercise your rights with Google directly: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can also contact tokenlimitgames@gmail.com with any question, or lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
