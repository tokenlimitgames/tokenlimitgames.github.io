Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Glitchling ("the game") is a virtual-pet game for Android made by Token Limit Games (Aleksandr Sandler), Lithuania. Contact: tokenlimitgames@gmail.com.

This policy explains what data the game and the services it uses handle, and what choices you have.

The short version

  • The game has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your pet, Hall of Fame and collected features are stored only on your phone.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device data (such as the advertising ID) to show and measure ads. In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland you're asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in the game. In US states with privacy laws (such as California) you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your data for ads the same way.
  • The developer does not sell, receive or store your personal data.

Data stored on your phone

The game saves your pet's state, its name, the Hall of Fame, the features you've spotted, hint and settings counters, and similar game progress in the app's private storage on your device. It is never sent to the developer. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data. Android's own backup may include it if you have device backup turned on.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows a banner ad and optional video ads (watched in exchange for a hint or a revive). These are provided by Google AdMob. To serve, measure and prevent fraud in ads, Google may collect and process data from your device, such as:

  • the advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, app version),
  • interactions with ads.

If you consent (or where consent isn't required), ads may be personalized. If you don't consent, Google serves non-personalized ads, which still use limited data (for example for frequency capping and fraud prevention). Learn more in Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices:

  • EU, EEA, UK, Switzerland: you're asked for consent the first time you open the game. You can change it any time via the privacy settings in the game.
  • US states with privacy laws: you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for ads via the PRIVACY button in the game (see below).
  • Everywhere: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Buying Glitchling Max removes the ads.

Google Play services

  • In-app purchases (Glitchling Max and device shells) are handled by Google Play. The developer receives a confirmation of purchase, not your payment details.
  • Ratings: the game may show Google Play's rating card. The developer never learns whether or how you rated.
  • Google's handling of this data is described in Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Notifications

Care reminders ("your glitchling is hungry") are created on your phone by the game itself. No data is sent anywhere to produce them. You can turn them off in Android's settings.

Sharing pictures

When you tap Share or Save, the game creates a picture of your glitchling. It is saved to your gallery or passed to the app you choose only when you ask for it. The developer never receives it.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Ads are not intended for children.

Your rights (EU/EEA/UK)

Since the developer does not collect or store your personal data, there's nothing the developer holds about you. For data processed by Google (ads, Play), you can exercise your rights with Google directly: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can also contact tokenlimitgames@gmail.com with any question, or lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Your rights (US states)

Residents of California and other US states with privacy laws (such as Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah and Texas) have rights over their personal information.

  • What is involved: the developer collects nothing itself. Through the ads, Google receives identifiers (the advertising ID), approximate location derived from the IP address, and internet or other activity (how ads are shown, watched and tapped), from your device, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud.
  • Sale and sharing: the developer doesn't sell your data for money. Under some state laws, letting an ad network use this data for personalized ads may still count as "selling" or "sharing" it for targeted advertising.
  • Opt out: tap the PRIVACY button in the game and choose not to allow the sale or sharing of your personal information. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's settings.
  • Other rights: you can ask what personal information is processed, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Since the developer holds none, such requests are handled by Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can also contact tokenlimitgames@gmail.com.
  • You won't be treated differently, or get a worse game, for using these rights.
  • The game is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly sell or share the personal information of anyone under 16.
