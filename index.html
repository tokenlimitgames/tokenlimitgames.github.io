Glitchling

Glitchling is a virtual pet written almost entirely by an over-confident AI, supervised by one tired human.

The AI insists there are no bugs, only features. We decided to believe it.

Keep your glitchling fed, clean and happy, and collect every feature it ships with. Any resemblance to working software is purely coincidental.

Bubblodile

Remember the water toy where you pumped a button and marbles floated up to a hungry mouth? Bubblodile puts it on your phone.

Pump the water, steer the marbles by tilting the phone, and feed all eight to the creature before it spits them back out. Shake it and everything swirls.

Beat your own records for the fastest time and the fewest pumps, then try the piranha, the frog, the octopus or the whale.
