SPECIMEN QUOTA

// PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Specimen Quota ("the game") is a comedy strategy game for Android made by Token Limit Games (Aleksandr Sandler), Lithuania. Contact: tokenlimitgames@gmail.com.

This policy explains what data the game and the services it uses handle, and what choices you have.

The short version

  • The game has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your contract in progress, your campaign progress and your settings are stored only on your phone.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device data (such as the advertising ID) to show and measure ads. In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland you're asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in the game. In US states with privacy laws (such as California) you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your data for ads the same way.
  • The developer does not sell, receive or store your personal data.

Data stored on your phone

The game saves the contract you're playing, which contracts you've completed and your best ratings, and your settings (game speed, sound on or off, which tips you've already seen), your service record (totals, best results and commendations) and which Field Manual pages you've opened, in the app's private storage on your device. It is never sent to the developer. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data. Android's own backup may include it if you have device backup turned on.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the game creates a picture of your result or service record, with a short text. It is passed only to the app you choose, only when you ask for it. The developer never receives it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows a banner ad, provided by Google AdMob. To serve, measure and prevent fraud in ads, Google may collect and process data from your device, such as:

  • the advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, app version),
  • interactions with ads.

If you consent (or where consent isn't required), ads may be personalized. If you don't consent, Google serves non-personalized ads, which still use limited data (for example for frequency capping and fraud prevention). Learn more in Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices:

  • EU, EEA, UK, Switzerland: you're asked for consent the first time you open the game. You can change it any time: tap the wrench (Settings) → "Privacy options".
  • US states with privacy laws: you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for ads the same way: the wrench (Settings) → "Privacy options" (see below).
  • Everywhere: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).

In-app purchases

The game currently has no in-app purchases. If any are added (for example to remove ads), they will be handled by Google Play, the developer will receive a confirmation of purchase but never your payment details, and this policy will be updated first.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Ads are not intended for children.

Your rights (EU/EEA/UK)

Since the developer does not collect or store your personal data, there's nothing the developer holds about you. For data processed by Google (ads, Play), you can exercise your rights with Google directly: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can also contact tokenlimitgames@gmail.com with any question, or lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Your rights (US states)

Residents of California and other US states with privacy laws (such as Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah and Texas) have rights over their personal information.

  • What is involved: the developer collects nothing itself. Through the ads, Google receives identifiers (the advertising ID), approximate location derived from the IP address, and internet or other activity (how ads are shown and tapped), from your device, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud.
  • Sale and sharing: the developer doesn't sell your data for money. Under some state laws, letting an ad network use this data for personalized ads may still count as "selling" or "sharing" it for targeted advertising.
  • Opt out: tap the wrench (Settings) → "Privacy options" in the game and choose not to allow the sale or sharing of your personal information. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's settings.
  • Other rights: you can ask what personal information is processed, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Since the developer holds none, such requests are handled by Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can also contact tokenlimitgames@gmail.com.
  • You won't be treated differently, or get a worse game, for using these rights.
  • The game is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly sell or share the personal information of anyone under 16.
